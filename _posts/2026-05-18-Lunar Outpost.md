<h3>Description and Outcome</h3>



I am currently working as an electrical engineering intern at <a href="https://www.lunaroutpost.com/" target="_blank" title="Lunar Outpost">Lunar Outpost</a>, a NASA subcontractor. Lunar Outpost is a company focused on establishing a permanent human presence on the moon and is developing several rovers that will be deployed for Artemis, and the Australian Space Agency. 

My role includes assisting in the development and testing of PCBAs responsible for the power distribution aboard the MAPP (Mobile Autonomous Prospecting Platform) Rover. This involves simulations, designing and executing tests, and working in Altium to ensure the boards are reliable and can withstand the incredible thermal and radiation environment of the lunar surface. 

I am also working on some of the communications infrastructure, including the radio carrier boards that will enable communication between the lunar rover and Mission Control.  

Additionally, the other interns and I created a scale, remote controllable model of the LV3 MAPP rover, complete with all the sensors to be incorporated into the final rover, to be used both to demonstrate functionality and as a test bed for the GNC (Guidance, Navigation, and Control) team. I was responsible for the electrical architecture, including the power system and connection to the sensors and actuators.
